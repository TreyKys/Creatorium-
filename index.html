/02 — PRODUCTS & SERVICES

Meet the Fab 4

Four apps, one mission: turn the chaos on your phone into signal.

01

Magnum Opus

Personal Knowledge Vault

Put all your reading, notes, and audio in one place—then just ask it questions.

Magnum Opus is a private workspace built to handle information overload. Instead of getting buried under a mountain of unread documents, long web articles, and audio files, drop them into a single secure space and instantly pull out exactly what you need.

  • Multi-Format Import — scanned PDFs, web article links, long voice recordings: everything is read, indexed, and instantly searchable.
  • Interactive Document Chat — talk directly to your files; get summaries, specifics, and connecting ideas across documents at once.
  • Focus & Energy Dials — dial complexity down for plain-English answers when you’re exhausted, or up for a detailed breakdown.
  • Private Storage — documents, scans, and chat histories live on your own device. Your library stays yours.
02

Sift

Smart Screenshot Ingestion & Optimization

Search your screenshots. Clean the clutter.

Sift is an intelligent, privacy-first screenshot management platform that transforms your chaotic camera roll into an instantly searchable, self-optimizing database. Running a high-performance local Isar NoSQL database, Sift reads, indexes, and organizes your media entirely in the background.

  • Semantic Tag Engine — local OCR plus lightweight LLM processing auto-categorizes and indexes screenshots by their actual contents.
  • Conversational Search — locate receipts, code snippets, or old conversations just by describing what you remember.
  • Smart Purge & De-duplication — a dedicated sweep that clears near-duplicates, expired temp shots, and junk memes to keep storage pristine.
03

Context

The Vibe Translator

Decode the internet in real-time.

Context is a frictionless, on-screen overlay dictionary designed to bridge the gap between static definitions and fluid internet culture. Built with Flutter and powered by Firebase Vertex AI, Context runs a floating background service that analyzes on-screen text on the fly—without leaving your current app.

  • Vibe Translation — LLM semantic analysis decodes slang, cultural references, and memes that standard dictionaries miss.
  • Floating Screen Overlay — instant contextual lookups via an unobtrusive floating window over WhatsApp, X, or any browser.
  • Hybrid Knowledge Base — generative AI synthesis backed by classical dictionary APIs and Wikipedia fallbacks for rock-solid accuracy.
04

Opinions NG

LIVE — FLAGSHIP

Where Nigeria calls it before it happens.

Our biggest product, live today. Opinions NG is a peer-to-peer forecasting and opinion marketplace built to handle sports and current-affairs pools cleanly—featuring quick local payment checkouts and automatic result updates.

  • Peer-to-Peer Pools — clean, transparent forecasting markets for sports and current affairs.
  • Local Payment Checkouts — fast, familiar Nigerian payment rails, in and out.
  • Automatic Result Updates — outcomes resolve and settle without manual drama.

05

Integro Ecosystem

IN DEVELOPMENT

High-integrity peer-to-peer infrastructure.

Integro is a heavy-duty decentralized trust engine and peer-to-peer financial network built natively on the high-performance Hedera network. By combining smart-contract asset backing with decentralized lending primitives and offline accessibility, Integro provides an all-in-one ecosystem for trustless asset trading, liquidity allocation, and peer-to-peer financing.

  • Smart Lending Pools — securely deposit liquidity, monitor active terms, and borrow against tokenized assets via code-enforced pools (LendingPool.sol).
  • Immutable Escrow Architecture — programmatic market escrow that locks, verifies, and settles peer-to-peer listings without middle-tier institutions (Escrow.sol).
  • USSD Mobile Bridging — an embedded USSD simulator layer proves ledger interaction over basic telecom channels: decentralized finance, completely offline, on low-end feature phones.
  • Native Hedera Token Integration — micro-fee, high-throughput minting, asset indexing, and security staking on Hedera’s token architecture (AssetToken.sol).
06

Conversational Ledger (LedgerX)

Voice-First WhatsApp Bookkeeping

Zero-interface bookkeeping for fast-paced commerce.

Conversational Ledger is a zero-UI transactional ledger operating natively inside WhatsApp. Engineered for merchants who can’t afford to stop and type out receipts, the platform uses a text and voice-first pipeline to process raw conversational notes into immaculate, structured financial ledgers on the fly.

  • Naija Voice Core (ASR & Phonetics) — a specialized speech-recognition framework and phonetic lexicon fine-tuned to parse localized speech, marketplace slang, and colloquial financial numbers straight from voice notes.
  • Natural Language Parsing Engine — deconstructs raw text and transcriptions into structured digital slips: transaction types, exact currency amounts, product categories.
  • Frictionless WhatsApp Pipeline — listens to chat webhooks, validates, updates accounting state, and returns instant confirmations and balances. No external app required.
  • Relational Ledger Storage — a transactional schema handling robust balance tracking, real-time historic audits, and market data records.
